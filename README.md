<h1 align="center">Latent</h1>

When shooting analog, a latent image typically refers to the invisible chemical impression formed on film the instant light strikes the silver halide crystals. Whilst every highlight, shadow and expression exists in that instant, it remains invisible until it enters the chemical developer bath.

After two decades of shooting digital, I, like many others, have drawers of multiple external drives holding tens of thousands of photographs. These unculled and sometimes uncaptioned drives are the modern day latent images; decades of craft waiting in the dark to be rediscovered.

This is why I built `Latent`, a digital developer. It doesn't upload your memories to the cloud (unless you ask it too). Instead it sits on your Mac, and uses local models (including Apple Intelligence) to catalog, caption and critique your images.
